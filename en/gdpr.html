🔒 Exercising your GDPR rights

Under the GDPR, you can request deletion of your account, an export of your data, or correction of your information. We'll reply within 30 days.

💡 From the app, you can do the following directly:
• Delete your account → Settings → Delete my account
• Export your data → Progress → Export my data
• Edit your profile → Account settings

Your data is used only to handle your request. A reply is guaranteed within 30 days.
