1. Purpose and acceptance

These Terms govern the use of the PauseCafé mobile app and the pausecafe-app.fr website, published by Kévin Beguerie, a private individual, France. By using PauseCafé, you accept these Terms without reservation.

2. Description of the service

  • Daily tracking of caffeine intake in mg
  • Daily hydration tracking in glasses of water (dedicated counter, adjustable goal from 4 to 12 glasses a day)
  • Active caffeine — a real-time estimate of the caffeine present in the body (residual caffeine), shown on iPhone, in the widget and on Apple Watch. A Premium
  • A progression system with points, badges, levels and streaks
  • Personalised trend statistics (separate ☕ Caffeine and 💧 Water tabs)
  • Rewards: partner promo codes for everyone, physical gifts or gift vouchers / discount vouchers of equivalent value for Premium subscribers, and exchanging points for Premium time for free users
  • A referral system for inviting friends
  • Caution mode — automatically sets the caffeine limit to 200 mg a day (EFSA benchmark) AND the hydration goal to 9 glasses / 2.3 L (WHO benchmark for pregnant and breastfeeding women)
  • An iOS widget available to all users (concentric caffeine + hydration rings)
  • A personalised onboarding that configures the experience around your habits (caffeine + hydration)
  • Community leaderboard, community challenges and friends system (usernames only)
  • Weekly caffeine report — an email sent every Monday at 8:30am with your stats for the week (can be switched on or off in Settings → Notifications → Emails)
  • Smart notifications (Premium) — hydration reminders tailored to your day (around 10am, 1pm, 4pm), an alert as you approach your caffeine limit, and a “late coffee” warning when a coffee is likely to still be with you at bedtime; each can be switched on or off individually
  • Re-engagement reminders — local reminder notifications if the app goes unused for several days (3, 7 then 30 days), which can be switched on or off (free)
  • Apple Health integration (HealthKit) — optional writing of caffeine and water to Apple Health; reading of sleep and resting heart rate (Premium, see section 6bis)
  • Apple Watch syncing (caffeine + water) — for Premium subscribers only
  • An optional Premium subscription

PauseCafé is intended for people aged 9 and over. The app requires iOS 16 or later. Apple Watch requires an active Premium subscription.

5. Profile data and personalisation

On first use (onboarding), the app invites you to provide several optional pieces of information:

  • Your usual number of caffeinated drinks per day
  • Your main caffeine tracking goal
  • Your daily hydration goal (from 4 to 12 glasses, with a sensible default depending on caution mode)
  • The usual time of your first caffeinated drink
  • Your weight (optional) — used to refine your sleep threshold and caffeine goal. It is stored on your device only and is never sent to our servers.
This information is entirely optional. It is used solely to personalise the experience and the notifications. You can change it at any time in the settings. It is never shared or sold.

  • Points have no monetary value

9.1 Daily limit on drinks eligible for points

📊 Anti-abuse — fairness of the system: to keep the reward system fair and prevent misuse (points farming), only the first 10 drinks logged per calendar day (from 00:00 to 23:59 in your device's local time zone) are eligible for points. This limit applies to all entries logged (caffeinated drinks and glasses of water).

Beyond this daily limit:
  • Health tracking (caffeine in mg, hydration in glasses, statistics) continues as normal.
  • No points (drinks, or badges based on the number of drinks) are awarded.
  • The counter resets automatically at midnight, local time zone.

Other cases where points are not awarded: caffeinated drinks logged beyond the daily mg limit (400 mg in standard mode, 200 mg in EFSA caution mode) earn no points, but are still counted in health tracking to help you stay aware of your intake. Glasses of water are never capped by an mg limit (they contain no caffeine).

9.2 How water counts in badges and challenges

💧 How water is counted:
  • Water COUNTS as a drink for neutral counters (the “100 drinks”, “500 drinks” and “1000 drinks” badges, the “100 drinks” challenge, and the total number of entries).
  • Water is NEUTRAL for caffeine-specific badges (for example “no coffee in the afternoon”, “first coffee before 9am”, “tea only”, “reduced average intake”). Water does not invalidate these badges and is not counted in caffeine averages.
  • In the Premium statistics, an “Include water in stats” toggle lets you choose whether water appears in the breakdown by type, the hourly averages and the top drinks.

Right to modify: The publisher reserves the right to change the game mechanics (points scale, limits, cooldowns, badge semantics) without notice. These changes give rise to no entitlement to compensation.
