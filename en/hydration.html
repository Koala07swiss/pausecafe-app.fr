💧 Hydration tracking

Track your hydration

PauseCafé doesn't only track your caffeine: it also helps you drink enough water, without guilt-tripping you. Here's exactly what the app does today, what's coming, the science it rests on, what it analyses — and why “coffee dehydrates you” is a myth.

💧 What the app does today

Hydration tracking is free for everyone. It sits alongside caffeine tracking, around the same cup.

  • A dedicated water counter. Each glass is added in one tap via the “+ Water” button. The default unit is a 250 ml glass.
  • A personal goal. Adjustable from 4 to 12 glasses a day. The default is 8 glasses, or 2 litres — the widely used benchmark, aligned with the EFSA adequate intake for an adult woman.
  • A blue ring. Around the cup, a blue ring fills as you progress and turns green once you reach your goal — much like activity rings.
  • Dedicated stats. In the 💧 Water tab of My Progress: your average, your consistency, your records and how your intake is spread across the day.
  • Caution mode. During pregnancy or breastfeeding, your goal automatically moves to 9 glasses (2.3 L) — the WHO recommendation.

The hydration reminders — nudges to drink during the day (around 10am, 1pm, 4pm), taking into account what you've already drunk so you're not prompted needlessly — are part of PauseCafé Premium.

💧 “If it can be counted, it should be”

An option lets you include water in your overall stats, or not. The principle: anything that can be counted is counted by default, for a complete picture of your habits — and you can always turn it off.

🌡️ Weather-aware hydration Available

In hot weather or a heatwave, your water needs increase — PauseCafé alerts you and adapts its reminders accordingly.

  • Based on Météo-France alert levels. In France, the app reads the heat alert level for your département to adapt its messages.
  • Département chosen manually in the app — no geolocation, and a neutral fallback if the information isn't available.
  • Free alert for everyone : an alert banner appears on the home screen and the widget. The stepped-up hydration reminders during hot weather are part of Premium.
  • Wellness framing. Encouraging nudges, never medical instructions.

🔬 What this is based on

PauseCafé's hydration benchmarks rest on official European references, not on made-up figures.

The reference water intake

L'EFSA (2010 opinion) defines an adequate intake of total water de 2.0 L/day for a woman et 2.5 L/day for a man, under moderate temperature and activity conditions. The key point: this is total water — roughly 80% comes from drinks (of all kinds) and 20% from food. During pregnancy, EFSA adds around +300 ml/day; while breastfeeding, around +700 ml/day.

💡 Why “8 glasses / 2 L” is a benchmark, not a rule

The famous “8 glasses a day” is a practical benchmark, not a universal truth. Your actual needs depend on your size, your activity, the climate and what you eat. PauseCafé therefore suggests 2 L by default (aligned with the EFSA adult-woman value) but lets you adjust from 4 to 12 glasses : the goal is a motivating guide, not a prescription.

The “coffee dehydrates you” myth

Many people believe every coffee must be “offset” with water, because caffeine is supposedly dehydrating. The science says otherwise: caffeine is a mild diuretic, but at moderate doses (up to around 400 mg/day) this effect is offset by the liquid in the drink itself. A controlled study (Killer et al., 2014) compared coffee and water in 50 regular drinkers: no difference across a wide range of hydration markers. The conclusion: drunk in moderation, coffee hydrates about as well as water in habitual drinkers.

What this means for the app. PauseCafé counts water separately not to “cancel out” coffee, but to encourage you to keep a good plain-water habit alongside your caffeinated drinks. We're not demonising coffee — we're just helping you find a balance.

🚫 What the app does not do

🔢

Impose a universal number

There's no “you must drink X litres” that holds for everyone. Your needs vary; the goal is adjustable and indicative.

🩺

Give medical advice

PauseCafé is a wellness app. If you have a condition (kidney, heart) or any doubt, your doctor decides.

⚠️

Push you to drink too much

Drinking to excess isn't harmless (hyponatraemia at the extreme). The goal aims at a healthy benchmark, not a record.

🔒

Exploit your health data

Your weight (used for some benchmarks) stays on your device. No health data is ever sold.

⚠️ Wellness information

PauseCafé's hydration benchmarks and analyses are provided for guidance and have no medical value: neither a medical device nor medical advice. For any question about your water needs or your health, consult a healthcare professional.
