Unsubscribe from emails

You unsubscribe directly in the app, in 3 steps. It takes effect immediately.

1
Open the app PauseCafé on your iPhone
2
Go to My account → Settings → Notifications → Emails
3
Turn off “App emails” (or just “Monday report” if you want to keep the others)

💡 Why this way? It's immediate and updates your preferences straight away. No waiting, no form to fill in.

No longer have access to the app? Write to us at hello@pausecafe-app.fr

Security emails (verification, account deletion, gift dispatch) always remain active.
