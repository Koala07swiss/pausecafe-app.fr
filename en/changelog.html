1.3.0 July 2026

A major release: hydration has been rebuilt from the ground up, drinks now come in sizes, and PauseCafé adapts to the weather.

💧 Hydration rebuilt
  • Everything moves to millilitres — counter, history, statistics, goal, reminders and the summary email. More precise than the old glasses.
  • The water in your drinks is counted — a coffee, a tea or a soda also provide water, and PauseCafé takes that into account automatically. You can turn it off in the settings.
☕ Drink sizes
  • Choose the size (S, M, L) — the volume and caffeine adjust accordingly each time you log a drink.
🌡️ Weather and heatwaves
  • Heatwave alert — in France, PauseCafé reads the Météo-France alert level for the département you choose (no geolocation). An alert banner appears on the home screen and the widget during hot weather, free for everyone.
  • Stepped-up hydration reminders — during a heatwave, your reminders step up automatically (Premium).
  • Annotated hydration chart — very hot days are flagged on your chart, so you can read your habits in context.
⌚ Apple Watch (Premium)
  • New Hydration complication on your watch face, alongside caffeine.
  • More reliable syncing — a drink logged from the Watch is never lost again.
🔧 Fixes and quality of life
  • The weekly email summary is now optional (off by default).
  • The water shown in the widget now matches your actual total for the day exactly.
  • Better data isolation when switching accounts on the same device.
  • Various reliability improvements.

1.2.0 June 2026

PauseCafé's flagship feature arrives: active caffeine.

✨ What's new
  • Real-time active caffeine — see the estimated amount of caffeine still in your body: it rises with each drink then falls away over the hours. At a glance, you know how much will be left at bedtime. Available in the app, in the widget, on Apple Watch and as complications on your watch face.
  • Syncing with the Health app — write your caffeine and water to the Health app to find them alongside the rest of your data.
  • Premium insights: caffeine, sleep and heart — discover the link between your caffeine and your actual sleep, and between your caffeine and your resting heart rate. The analyses use Health app data and are computed directly on your device: they never leave your iPhone.
